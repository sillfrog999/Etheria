# PROJECT ETHERIA — GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD)

## 1. VISION & CORE PHILOSOPHY
Project Etheria is a persistent, browser-based, isometric 2D Sandbox MMORPG.
*"The world does not revolve around the player. The player creates their own place in the world."*

Players possess full autonomy:
- Choose from 4 foundational combat classes: **Mage**, **Fighter**, **Assassin**, **Ranger**.
- Master 15 distinct skills across Gathering, Crafting, Combat, and Utility.
- Harvest real, regenerating natural resources across interconnected regions.
- Trade in a secure two-phase exchange with atomic currency transfers.
- Buy and sell equipment on the Aurelia central marketplace.
- Delve into dungeons and battle dangerous open-world monsters and bosses.

---

## 2. WORLD REGIONS & GEOGRAPHY
- **Greenfall (Level 1+)**: Safe starter grassland, rivers, basic copper and tin ore, novice wildlife and bandit scouts.
- **Aurelia (Central City)**: Sanctuary safe zone with bank vaults, artisan crafting forges, guild headquarters, and public marketplace.
- **Silverwood (Level 10+)**: Ancient forest rich in ancient timber, rare alchemical flora, and mysterious ruins.
- **Ashen Mountains (Level 20+)**: Craggy volcanic foothills yielding iron, coal, and mithril veins.
- **Frostmere (Level 35+)**: Glacial tundra with silver deposits, ice crystals, and dangerous frost wolves.
- **Emberlands (Level 50+)**: Volcanic wasteland containing raw obsidian deposits and full PvP hazard rules.
- **The Void (Level 70+)**: Endgame dimension characterized by chaos, void essence, and the legendary Void Titan world boss.

---

## 3. COMBAT CLASSES
1. **Mage**: Spellmaster specializing in Pyromancy, Cryomancy, and Restoration. High mana pool, devastating burst damage, and tactical teleportation.
2. **Fighter**: Frontline vanguard armed with shields, broadswords, and battleaxes. Boasts high health, damage reduction, and counterattacks.
3. **Assassin**: Master of stealth, evasion, and critical strikes. Exploits shadow stepping and backstabbing to eliminate high-value targets.
4. **Ranger**: Precision marksman utilizing bows and flintlock rifles. Features tactical rolls and piercing area attacks.

---

## 4. SKILL PROGRESSION (MAX LEVEL 99)
Skills advance via action-based experience (classic RuneScape XP progression curve):
- **Gathering**: Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing, Farming, Hunting
- **Crafting**: Smithing, Crafting, Cooking, Alchemy
- **Combat**: Attack, Strength, Defence, Magic, Ranged
- **Utility**: Agility
